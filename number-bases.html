How do number bases work?

Same number, different places

Each step to the left makes a place 2 times as big. A digit tells you how many groups of that size to use.

A 0 keeps a place empty. Zeros at the front do not change the number. All place values and totals shown here are in base 10.

A worked example

If a digit is too small, the places on the right may not hold enough. If it is too big, you pass the target. Start again and try a different digit.
