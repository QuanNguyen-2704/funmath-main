How to play

  1. Choose a rectangle of empty squares.
  2. Its area must be a power of 2. One square is allowed: 20 = 1.
  3. Check the preview, then press Shade rectangle.
  4. Pass the turn. Shade the last square and you lose!

Example: a 2 × 4 rectangle has area 8 = 2³. A 3 × 2 rectangle has area 6, so it is not allowed.
